Setting up alerts

This page cannot send alerts on its own. These are the steps that do.

  1. Ask your loan officer for a text or email when your quoted rate moves 0.125% or more, and for a same-day quote whenever you ask.
  2. Add a quote to the log every Thursday. Freddie Mac publishes its weekly survey that morning.
  3. On days with CPI, jobs or Fed speaker releases, expect same-day swings. If your lock decision is close, ask for the quote after 10:00 AM.
  4. Put "last day to float" from the top of this page on your calendar with a reminder one week earlier.
  5. Ask whether the lender offers a float-down option and what it costs. It lets you lock now and still capture a later drop.
